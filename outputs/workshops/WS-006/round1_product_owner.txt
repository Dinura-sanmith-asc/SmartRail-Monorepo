As the Product Owner Stakeholder Agent for SmartRail, here is my independent position for Round 1 regarding **Driver Pre/Post-Journey Workflows, Precision Stopping, and External Roles**:

### 1. What matters from your stakeholder perspective
* **Safety & Regulatory Compliance:** Ensuring the train is securely shut down and handed over without ambiguity. Safety-critical procedures (like post-journey securement) cannot be bypassed.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Reducing cognitive load and administrative friction for drivers during pre- and post-journey handovers while maintaining auditability.
* **MVP Boundary Control:** Preventing scope creep. SmartRail must deliver high-value core capabilities without getting bogged down in building enterprise systems for external roles or complex real-time telemetry processing that belongs in adjacent railway systems.

### 2. What responsibility you believe your role has
* Defining clear operational rules of precedence and standard workflows that align with railway regulations and safety cases.
* Prioritizing business capabilities that deliver the highest ROI in safety, punctuality, and reduced dwell/turnaround times.
* Determining what is strictly in-scope for the MVP versus what must be deferred to future phases or integrated via existing enterprise interfaces.

### 3. What responsibility your role should NOT have
* I am *not* here to design the software architecture, data pipelines, UI mockups, or database schemas. 
* I am *not* responsible for defining how external operational systems (e.g., maintenance or signaller systems) internally process their data, only *what* data needs to flow in and out of SmartRail via interfaces.

### 4. What risks or failure cases concern you
* **"Single Source of Truth" paralysis:** If on-board data conflicts with Control or paper, and the system lacks a legally sound, operationally approved hierarchy, it could cause dangerous operational delays or driver hesitation.
* **Workflow Gridlock:** Enforcing a rigid DMI post-journey checklist that cannot handle exceptional operational scenarios (e.g., emergency relief drivers or degraded stock) could trap the train in an un-cleared state.
* **Over-Scope Creep:** Trying to build direct in-app workflows for external roles (Shunters, Duty Managers, Medical personnel) within SmartRail will bloat the assessment scope and dilute the core driver-centric value proposition.

### 5. What decision you believe needs clarification
* **Precedence Hierarchy:** We need an explicit, organizationally approved ruling on source precedence (typically: Signaller/Control voice or signalled authority > On-board safety systems > Paper/Schedules).
* **External Role Integration Boundaries:** We must formally decide whether external roles (Control, Shunters, Maintenance) interact *through* SmartRail as users or *via* established external channels and legacy systems.

### 6. Any assumption you disagree with
* **Assumption to challenge (CF-035):** The assumption that SmartRail needs to provide *dynamic braking guides for precision stopping* for the MVP. While a nice-to-have, precision stopping is a complex, high-risk feature heavily dependent on rolling stock specifics and signaling integrations. For the MVP, basic stopping guidance or standard timetable adherence should suffice unless mandated by safety regulations.
* **Assumption to challenge (CF-040):** The assumption that external operational roles need direct touchpoints within SmartRail. For our scope, these roles should remain external actors whose authorizations are logged or communicated via Control, rather than building multi-user operational modules for maintenance and medical staff inside this product.
