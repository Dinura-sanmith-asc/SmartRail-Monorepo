# Passenger Interview

As the Passenger Stakeholder Agent, I've walked through a complete railway journey, from initial planning to reaching the destination and post-journey considerations. My primary focus is to ensure passengers experience minimum confusion, receive accurate information, understand disruptions, and recover effectively when things go wrong.

---

### **Stage 1: Planning the Journey (Pre-booking/Pre-travel)**

This is where the journey begins, often weeks or days before departure. Passengers are exploring options.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Routes & Schedules:** Available routes, journey durations, train frequencies, first/last train times between my origin and destination.
    *   **Pricing & Fares:** Transparent cost estimates for different ticket types (e.g., peak/off-peak, advance, flexible, season tickets), and how railcards apply.
    *   **Transfers:** If the journey isn't direct, I need to know transfer points, estimated connection times, and if specific tickets cover the full multi-leg journey.
    *   **Accessibility:** Detailed information for both stations and trains (e.g., step-free access, accessible toilets, assistance services, ramp requirements).
    *   **On-board Services:** Availability of WiFi, catering, power outlets, quiet zones, luggage space (especially for large items, bikes, prams).
    *   **Station Facilities:** Information about parking, local public transport links at origin/destination, and any specific station amenities.
    *   **Disruptions:** Any known planned engineering works or major long-term disruptions affecting my potential travel dates.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Find the most convenient, cost-effective, and suitable route and time for my needs.
    *   Compare various options easily.
    *   Ensure the journey meets specific requirements (e.g., step-free access, space for a wheelchair/pram, bike carriage).
    *   Accurately estimate my arrival time for onward plans.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Information Overload/Scarcity:** Difficulty finding comprehensive, consistent information across different operators or being redirected multiple times.
    *   **Confusing Fares:** Unclear fare structures, hidden costs, or difficulty understanding which ticket type is best value for my specific circumstances (e.g., commuting vs. leisure).
    *   **Inaccurate Accessibility Data:** Booking a journey only to find advertised accessibility features are non-existent or inadequate.
    *   **Unforeseen Issues:** Not being aware of planned engineering works until after booking, leading to unexpected changes or cancellations.
    *   **Connection Uncertainty:** Confusion about whether connections are protected or if there's enough time to change trains.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Unified Journey Planner:** A single, national platform that aggregates all routes, schedules, and real-time prices from all operators.
    *   **Transparent Fares:** Clearly display all ticket types, their terms, and the total cost upfront, including railcard discounts.
    *   **Detailed Accessibility:** Integrate comprehensive, verified accessibility information for every station and type of rolling stock, allowing filtering by specific needs (e.g., "step-free platform access," "accessible toilet on train").
    *   **Proactive Disruption Warnings:** Highlight planned engineering works or long-term disruptions upfront and suggest suitable alternatives.
    *   **Service Details:** Display information on available on-board services (WiFi, catering, power sockets) and estimated occupancy levels for trains.
    *   **Integrated Onward Travel:** Provide basic information about local transport links at destination stations.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   How frequently will engineering work schedules be updated and verified by SmartRail from all operators?
    *   What level of granular detail will "accessibility information" provide? Will it include specific measurements (door widths, ramp gradients) or just general "accessible" tags?
    *   Will SmartRail provide *predictive* occupancy levels based on historical data and current booking trends, or just current real-time data? How reliable will this be?
    *   How will SmartRail handle scenarios where an optimal journey involves travel with multiple operators that don't traditionally integrate their ticketing?

### **Stage 2: Booking and Purchasing Tickets**

Once a journey is selected, the passenger moves to securing their travel.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Final Confirmation:** A clear summary of the selected journey (date, time, stations, number of passengers, ticket type).
    *   **Total Cost:** The precise, final cost, including any seat reservation fees or other add-ons.
    *   **Payment & Receipt:** Secure payment options and clear confirmation of purchase, with a digital receipt.
    *   **Ticket Delivery:** How I will receive my ticket (e.g., mobile ticket, print-at-home, collect at station) and clear instructions for each method.
    *   **Terms & Conditions:** A summary of the refund, exchange, and compensation policy for the purchased ticket.
    *   **Special Assistance Confirmation:** A confirmation that any requested assistance has been registered and will be provided.
    *   **Seat Selection:** Options to select specific seats (window/aisle, forward/backward, table, quiet zone, accessible space).

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Secure my chosen journey quickly and securely.
    *   Apply any discounts (e.g., railcard) seamlessly.
    *   Receive my ticket in a convenient format.
    *   Ensure any specific needs (e.g., special assistance, specific seat type) are accommodated and confirmed.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Technical Glitches:** The website/app crashing during payment, leading to uncertainty about purchase status.
    *   **Confusing Process:** A cluttered or non-intuitive checkout process, making it difficult to review details or apply discounts.
    *   **Ticket Delivery Failure:** Not receiving a ticket confirmation or the ticket itself, causing anxiety pre-travel.
    *   **Incorrect Booking:** Accidentally booking the wrong date, time, or route due to poor UI design.
    *   **Assistance Missed:** Special assistance requests not being correctly linked to the booking or confirmed.
    *   **Seat Frustration:** Inability to select suitable seats, or finding out the chosen seat type isn't guaranteed.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Seamless Booking Flow:** A secure, intuitive booking interface with clear steps and pre-filled data (e.g., from stored passenger profiles).
    *   **Visual Seat Maps:** Allow passengers to select seats from a visual coach layout, highlighting amenities (power sockets, accessible spaces, proximity to toilets).
    *   **Integrated Railcards:** Automatic application of registered railcard discounts.
    *   **Assistance Integration:** A dedicated section to request and confirm special assistance directly within the booking process, with a clear confirmation message.
    *   **Flexible Ticket Delivery:** Multiple options for ticket delivery (mobile wallet, email, print, station collection), with clear instructions for each.
    *   **Instant Confirmation:** Immediate, detailed booking confirmation via email and app notification.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   Will SmartRail act as a direct retailer for all train tickets, or will it redirect to individual operator sites for final purchase? If redirected, how will the seamless experience be maintained?
    *   How will SmartRail manage existing multi-operator fare structures and potential split-ticketing options to ensure passengers get the best value?
    *   What protocols will be in place for data security and privacy when storing payment details and personal information?
    *   Will the system differentiate between different types of assistance needs (e.g., mobility, visual impairment, hearing impairment) and tailor the assistance request accordingly?

### **Stage 3: Preparing for Departure (Day of travel, pre-station arrival)**

The day of travel arrives, and passengers are getting ready to head to the station.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Real-time Journey Status:** Any last-minute delays, cancellations, or platform changes for my specific booked journey.
    *   **Assistance Confirmation:** Re-confirmation that my special assistance request is active and details of where to meet staff if required.
    *   **Travel to Station:** Current traffic conditions or local public transport status if I'm connecting to the station.
    *   **Station Info:** Expected platform number (if known early), and any station-specific advisories (e.g., lift out of order).
    *   **Weather:** Any relevant weather warnings that might impact travel.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Reconfirm all journey details and mentally prepare for travel.
    *   Ensure my ticket is easily accessible (e.g., phone charged, physical ticket ready).
    *   Plan my travel to the station to arrive punctually, especially if I need assistance.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Missed Updates:** Not receiving critical, last-minute information about delays or platform changes.
    *   **Assistance Failure:** Arriving at the station only to find my assistance request wasn't correctly logged or staff aren't present.
    *   **Ticket Troubles:** Forgetting my ticket or my phone battery dying with a mobile ticket.
    *   **Unexpected Delays:** Unforeseen issues (e.g., traffic, bus delays) on the way to the station, causing me to miss the train.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Proactive Notifications:** Push notifications for any significant delays, cancellations, or platform changes affecting my specific booked journey, with clear explanations.
    *   **'My Journeys' Section:** A dedicated area in the app/website showing the real-time status of all booked travel, with easy access to ticket details.
    *   **Assistance Check-in:** A way to confirm assistance requirements and receive contact details for staff.
    *   **Pre-emptive Reminders:** Reminders about ticket collection (if applicable) or ensuring mobile devices are charged for mobile tickets.
    *   **Integrated Local Transport:** Basic integration with local public transport or traffic apps to help plan travel to the station.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   What threshold constitutes a "significant" delay for a push notification (e.g., 5 min, 15 min)? Can passengers customize this?
    *   How will SmartRail ensure consistency of push notifications across all operators and different types of disruptions?
    *   Will SmartRail have an offline mode for displaying mobile tickets, in case of signal loss at the station?
    *   What mechanisms will be in place to ensure assistance staff are aware of incoming passengers, particularly if arrival times change?

---

### **Stage 4: At the Station (Pre-boarding)**

Passengers have arrived at the station and are preparing to board their train.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Platform & Status:** My train's precise platform number and real-time status (on time, delayed by X minutes, cancelled).
    *   **Coach Position:** Which coach my seat reservation is in, and where that coach will stop on the platform (critical for accessibility, luggage, or specific seat reservations).
    *   **Station Layout:** Location of facilities (toilets, waiting rooms, cafes, ticket office, assistance point, accessible routes like lifts/ramps).
    *   **Assistance Meeting Point:** Where to meet pre-booked assistance staff.
    *   **Station Disruptions:** Any station-specific issues (e.g., lift out of order, queuing times for gates).

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Find my platform quickly and safely, avoiding unnecessary detours.
    *   Locate my specific coach position to board efficiently.
    *   Confirm special assistance if needed.
    *   Monitor for further updates about my train, especially platform changes.
    *   Use station facilities if time allows.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Last-Minute Changes:** Sudden platform changes causing confusion and stress, particularly for passengers with reduced mobility, children, or heavy luggage.
    *   **Inaccurate Information:** Outdated or inconsistent information on station screens compared to my app.
    *   **Missing Staff:** Difficulty finding assistance staff despite pre-booking.
    *   **Platform Overcrowding:** Overcrowded platforms making navigation difficult and stressful.
    *   **Poor Orientation:** Not knowing where the accessible coach or my reserved seat will be on the platform, leading to unnecessary walking or rushing.
    *   **Facility Failure:** Lifts or escalators being out of service without prior warning.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Real-time Platform Updates:** Push real-time platform updates to the app, potentially even before station screens update.
    *   **Interactive Station Maps:** Provide interactive, GPS-enabled station maps showing facilities, assistance points, accessible routes, and *estimated coach stopping positions* on the platform.
    *   **Direct Assistance Contact:** A direct communication channel to station assistance staff (e.g., chat, call button).
    *   **Facility Alerts:** Real-time alerts for out-of-order station facilities (lifts, accessible toilets).
    *   **Crowd Density Information:** Where available, display crowd density on platforms or at ticket gates.
    *   **Audio/Visual Announcements:** Provide a text feed or audio playback of station announcements within the app, especially helpful for those with hearing impairments or in noisy environments.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   How will SmartRail integrate with potentially diverse station information systems (screens, PA systems) to ensure consistency and prevent conflicting information?
    *   What is the accuracy level of coach stopping position predictions, given varied train lengths and platform designs?
    *   How will SmartRail ensure that real-time lift/escalator status is consistently and accurately reported from all stations?
    *   Will the interactive station maps offer guidance for passengers with specific impairments (e.g., audio navigation for visually impaired passengers)?

### **Stage 5: On the Train**

The journey is underway, and passengers are en route to their destination.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Train Progress:** Current location of the train on a map, estimated arrival times at upcoming stops and the final destination.
    *   **Disruption Explanations:** Clear, timely explanations for any unexpected stops or delays.
    *   **On-board Facilities:** Real-time status of WiFi, catering, and toilet availability (especially accessible ones).
    *   **Next Stop Alerts:** Visual and audible alerts for upcoming stops.
    *   **Connecting Services:** Information about connecting services if my destination is an interchange point.
    *   **Emergency Information:** Emergency procedures and contact information.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Relax, work, or enjoy the journey without anxiety.
    *   Stay informed about journey progress and any changes.
    *   Use on-board facilities as needed.
    *   Prepare for my destination or connection.
    *   Discreetly report any non-emergency issues (e.g., cleanliness, anti-social behaviour).

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Information Blackout:** Lack of clear, timely information during unexpected stops or major delays, causing anxiety and confusion.
    *   **Facility Failures:** Discovering broken WiFi or unavailable catering after boarding, with no prior warning.
    *   **Missed Announcements:** Missing crucial announcements due to noise, distraction, or hearing impairment.
    *   **Disorientation:** Not knowing where the train currently is or how much longer the journey will take.
    *   **Seat Confusion:** Difficulty finding my reserved seat, or finding it occupied, with no easy way to resolve.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Real-time Train Tracking:** A live map showing the train's current location, speed, and estimated arrival times for all stops.
    *   **Proactive In-journey Updates:** Push notifications for delays or service disruptions affecting the current journey, with explanations (e.g., "signal fault at X location, expected 15 min delay").
    *   **On-board Facility Status:** Real-time updates on WiFi connectivity, catering availability, and toilet status (including accessible ones).
    *   **Next Stop Information:** Visual and optional audio announcements for next stops within the app, complementing on-board PIS.
    *   **Report an Issue:** A discreet feature to report non-emergency issues (e.g., heating problem, anti-social behaviour) directly to on-board staff.
    *   **Digital Seat Map:** A digital coach layout showing occupied/available seats to help locate reservations or find alternative seating.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   How will SmartRail collect and verify real-time status of on-board facilities (e.g., sensors, staff input)?
    *   What level of detail will be provided in disruption explanations (e.g., "technical fault" vs. specific detail)? How will this be communicated consistently across different operators?
    *   Will the app integrate with existing train PIS systems or require new data feeds?
    *   What is the service level agreement for responses to issues reported via the app?
    *   Will SmartRail provide information about other on-board services like quiet zones or designated family areas?

### **Stage 6: Interchange/Connection (if applicable)**

For journeys involving changes, this is a critical stage.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Platform Information:** Arrival platform of my current train and departure platform of my connecting train.
    *   **Connection Status:** Real-time status of the connecting train (on time, delayed, cancelled).
    *   **Connection Viability:** If my connection is still viable given any delays, and how much time I have.
    *   **Alternative Options:** Alternative connections if I miss my scheduled one, or if my connecting train is significantly delayed.
    *   **Assistance During Transfer:** Where to find assistance staff if needed for the transfer.
    *   **Station Navigation:** A clear map of the interchange station showing the route to the connecting platform, including accessible options.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Navigate safely and efficiently to my connecting platform.
    *   Confirm my connection is secure.
    *   Receive assistance if needed during the transfer.
    *   Have a clear backup plan if the connection is at risk or missed.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Missed Connection:** Delays on the first train making a tight connection impossible.
    *   **Navigation Confusion:** Not knowing which platform to go to, or long distances between platforms (especially with luggage or mobility issues).
    *   **Inaccessible Routes:** Discovering an accessible route between platforms is out of service or non-existent.
    *   **Lack of Support:** Difficulty finding staff to assist with missed connections or re-routing.
    *   **Unprotected Connection:** Missing a connection and having no clear guidance or support for onward travel.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Proactive Connection Alerts:** Alerts if a connection is at risk due to delays on my current train, with alternative options presented automatically.
    *   **Interactive Transfer Maps:** Station maps highlighting the most efficient and accessible route to the connecting platform, including estimated walking times.
    *   **Real-time Connection Status:** Continuously updated status of the connecting train, including its expected platform.
    *   **Missed Connection Guidance:** Clear instructions on what to do if a connection is missed, including re-booking options (if applicable) and how to contact staff.
    *   **Assistance Coordination:** Integration with assistance services to notify staff of passengers with tight connections or those who have requested transfer assistance.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   How will SmartRail standardize "minimum connection times" across different stations and operators, considering varying passenger mobility?
    *   Will SmartRail clearly indicate if a connection is "protected" (i.e., if I miss it due to railway fault, my next journey is covered)?
    *   What level of automatic re-booking will SmartRail facilitate in the event of a missed connection, especially if it involves multiple operators?
    *   How will the system ensure that information on accessible routes between platforms is accurate and real-time?

---

### **Stage 7: Arrival at Destination**

The train has reached its final stop, and passengers are disembarking.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Actual Arrival:** Confirmation of actual arrival time and platform.
    *   **Station Exits:** Location of exits, toilets, taxis, bus stops, tube/metro connections.
    *   **Assistance Confirmation:** Status of any pre-booked special assistance for disembarking.
    *   **Onward Travel:** Information about local public transport or onward travel options from the station.
    *   **Station Issues:** Any station-specific issues (e.g., lift out of order affecting a specific exit).
    *   **Lost Property:** Information on how to report lost property immediately.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Disembark safely and efficiently.
    *   Locate any specific luggage collection points (if applicable).
    *   Find my way out of the station or to onward transport easily.
    *   Receive special assistance if pre-arranged.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Missing Assistance:** Assistance staff not present upon arrival as expected, leading to difficulties disembarking or navigating.
    *   **Navigation Challenges:** Difficulty navigating a complex station, especially with luggage, children, or mobility impairments.
    *   **Inaccurate Onward Info:** Incorrect or outdated information about local public transport connections.
    *   **Facility Outages:** Discovering key facilities like lifts or escalators are out of service upon arrival.
    *   **Disorientation:** Exiting at the wrong side of the station, causing confusion for onward travel plans.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Arrival Confirmation:** Confirmation of arrival platform and estimated disembarkation time.
    *   **Interactive Destination Maps:** Interactive station maps highlighting exits, facilities, and local transport links (with real-time updates for buses/trams if possible).
    *   **Disembarkation Assistance:** Reminders and confirmation for disembarkation assistance, with a direct way to contact staff.
    *   **Lost Property Guidance:** Clear guidance or a direct link to report lost property.
    *   **Onward Travel Planning:** Integration with local public transport apps or ride-sharing services to facilitate onward journeys from the station.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   How will SmartRail ensure seamless coordination between train crew and station assistance staff for disembarkation, especially with variable arrival times?
    *   What level of real-time data integration will SmartRail have with local public transport providers (buses, trams, metro) to provide accurate onward journey information?
    *   Will the lost property feature be a centralized national system or simply redirect to individual operator websites?

---

### **Stage 8: Post-Journey (if issues arose)**

Even after reaching the destination, a passenger's journey isn't truly complete if issues occurred.

1.  **What you need to know:**
    *   **Compensation:** Clear instructions on how to claim compensation for delays or cancellations (Delay Repay).
    *   **Feedback/Complaints:** Easy way to provide feedback or make a complaint.
    *   **Lost Property:** How to track and retrieve lost property.
    *   **Journey Records:** Access to receipts or proof of travel for expenses.
    *   **Passenger Rights:** Clear information about my rights as a passenger.

2.  **What you want to do:**
    *   Seek appropriate compensation if eligible, without undue hassle.
    *   Provide feedback about my experience (positive or negative) to the correct operator.
    *   Retrieve any items I might have lost.
    *   Have an easily accessible record of my travel expenses.

3.  **What could go wrong:**
    *   **Confusing Claims:** A complex, confusing, or inconsistent compensation claims process across different operators.
    *   **Difficult Feedback:** Struggling to find the correct channel for feedback or complaints.
    *   **Lost Property Frustration:** A lengthy and frustrating process for tracing and retrieving lost items.
    *   **Missing Records:** Difficulty obtaining or accessing journey receipts for business expenses.

4.  **What SmartRail could reasonably help with:**
    *   **Simplified Compensation:** A centralized, simplified process for claiming Delay Repay, potentially auto-populating journey details from my SmartRail booking history.
    *   **Unified Feedback Portal:** A clear, national feedback and complaints portal that directs submissions to the correct operator while also providing aggregated data.
    *   **Central Lost Property Search:** A consolidated lost property search portal across all participating operators.
    *   **Digital Receipts:** Easily accessible digital journey receipts for all bookings made through SmartRail.
    *   **Passenger Rights Information:** A clear, concise section outlining passenger rights and entitlements.

5.  **What questions remain unanswered:**
    *   Will SmartRail directly manage compensation claims, or will it simply facilitate the process by redirecting to operators? How will consistency in outcomes be ensured?
    *   How will SmartRail handle disputes or appeals regarding compensation claims?
    *   What guarantees will there be for operators to respond to feedback/complaints submitted via SmartRail within a reasonable timeframe?
    *   Will the lost property portal require real-time updates from all operators, and how will its effectiveness be measured?
